# Assessment Information

## Project

FUTURE_CS_02 — Phishing Email Detection & Awareness System

## Internship

Future Interns Cyber Security Internship

## Task

Task 2 — Phishing Email Detection & Awareness System

## Assessment Date

10 October 2026

## Objective

Analyze email samples, identify phishing indicators, classify email risk,
explain phishing attacks in simple language, and create awareness and
prevention guidelines.

## Scope

The assessment was performed for educational and security-awareness purposes.

The analysis used passive/read-only inspection of email content and available
email header information.

No malicious links were intentionally clicked, no credentials were submitted,
and no suspicious attachments were executed.

## Samples

Email 01 and Email 03 are test phishing emails created with a fake mailer and
sent to the author's own Gmail inbox for awareness and educational purposes.
Email 02 is a legitimate email received from Internshala.

### Email 01
Classification: Phishing

Theme: Urgent account-lock notification ("Urgent: Your Account Will Be Locked")
with a "Verify Now" link, sent via fake mailer to Gmail

### Email 02
Classification: Safe

Theme: Internshala email (student@mail.internshala.com) about Google AI Plus
access; SPF, DKIM and DMARC passed

### Email 03
Classification: Suspicious

Theme: Generic account-suspension alert ("URGENT: Verify Your Account") with a
"Verify Account" button, sent via fake mailer to Gmail; Gmail warned that the
sender could not be verified

## Tools and Sources

- Fake mailer (used to create and send the test phishing emails)
- Gmail (inbox/spam view and raw email header data)
- Raw email header analysis (SPF / DKIM / DMARC)
- Browser/visual inspection
- Markdown
- PDF documentation

## Safety

Potentially malicious links and attachments were not opened or executed.
Recipient addresses, message identifiers, authentication signatures and
tracking tokens are not reproduced.
